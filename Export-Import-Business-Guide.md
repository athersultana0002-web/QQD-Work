# EXPORT-IMPORT BUSINESS GUIDE

## What is Export-Import?
The process of selling goods to foreign markets (Export) and purchasing goods from foreign markets (Import) to trade globally, expand business reach, and generate foreign exchange revenue.

## Licenses & Setup Required to Start in India
1.  **Company Registration:** Registered business entity (Pvt Ltd / LLP / Proprietorship) registered with MCA.
2.  **PAN:** Permanent Account Number for tax compliance.
3.  **Current Account:** Business bank account for international transactions.
4.  **IEC Code from DGFT (Main License):** A 10-digit Import Export Code issued by DGFT. Mandatory for all export and import activities.
5.  **GST Registration:** Mandatory for GST-compliant transactions.
6.  **Other Product-Specific Licenses:** FSSAI, APEDA, Drug License, etc.

## Export-Import Business Flow (6-Step Process)

| Step | Phase | Description |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 | **Product and Market** | Research product demand & target market analysis. |
| 2 | **Buyer & Supplier** | Identify reliable buyer/supplier via Alibaba, TradeIndia, trade fairs, LinkedIn. |
| 3 | **Order Confirm** | Finalize terms, pricing & Incoterms (EXW, FOB, CIF). |
| 4 | **Documents** | Invoice, packing list, bill of lading, and permits. |
| 5 | **Shipping & Customs** | Logistics & customs clearance (Shipping Bill / Bill of Entry). |
| 6 | **Payment** | Secure payment via LC, TT, or escrow. |

## Essential Documentation Guide
*   **Commercial Invoice:** Details the goods, price, buyer & seller info.
*   **Packing List:** Lists contents, weight, dimensions, packaging details.
*   **Bill of Lading:** Shipping contract & receipt for goods in transit.
*   **Certificate of Origin:** Proves the country where goods were manufactured.

## International Payment Methods
*   **Advance:** Buyer pays upfront (Lowest risk for seller).
*   **Letter of Credit / Bank Guarantee:** Bank assures payment on agreed terms.
*   **Documents Against Payment:** Documents released only after payment via bank.
*   **Open Account:** Buyer pays after receiving goods (Highest risk for seller).

## Main Risks & Mitigation Strategies
*   **Buyer Not Paying:** Mitigated by LC and ECGC Insurance.
*   **Currency Fluctuation:** Mitigated through forward contracts and FX hedging.
*   **Goods Damage:** Mitigated by Marine/Transit Insurance.
