# Audio 1: QQQ Workflow Transcription

Following assets and initial things are needed for QQQ export-import. From a functional angle, we'll first list it out and then the required systems can be developed for each functionality. The functional angle is coming straight from the Bhagirath's sessions.

## Required Steps & Process Flow

* **Licensing:** Acquire the required licenses.
* **Product Selection:** 
  * Research the product.
  * Finalize a product.
* **Marketing & Sales:**
  * Market that product.
  * Reach out to buyers.
  * Generate leads.
  * Generate responses.
  * Send out quotations for the leads.
  * Close the sales.
* **Financials & Supply Chain:**
  * Get the initial advance.
  * Find the suppliers.
  * Complete the freight forward, CHA (Customs House Agent), and the supply chain.
  * Track the delivery.
  * Get the remaining money.
